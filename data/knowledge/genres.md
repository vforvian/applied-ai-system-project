# Genre Guide

Each section is one retrievable chunk. The heading is the exact genre label used in
`data/songs.csv`. "Related" lists nearby styles and words listeners use, so a request
like "beats to study to" can find lofi songs.

## lofi
Related genres: lo-fi hip hop, chillhop, beats, ambient, jazz, study beats.
Known for: mellow beats, vinyl crackle, soft keys, relaxed loops for studying and focus.

## ambient
Related genres: atmospheric, drone, new age, chillout, space music, lofi, classical.
Known for: slow evolving textures, no drums or very soft drums, spacey and calm, meditation and sleep.

## jazz
Related genres: smooth jazz, swing, bossa nova, blues, soul, coffee shop, lounge.
Known for: improvisation, saxophone, piano, upright bass, warm acoustic sound.

## classical
Related genres: orchestral, piano, strings, chamber music, instrumental, quartet, ambient.
Known for: acoustic instruments, no vocals, emotional dynamics, focus and reflection.

## folk
Related genres: acoustic, country, americana, singer-songwriter, bluegrass, indie folk.
Known for: acoustic guitar, banjo, storytelling lyrics, warm and nostalgic sound.

## blues
Related genres: delta blues, soul, jazz, rock, r&b, americana.
Known for: guitar, emotional vocals, sad and soulful feeling, slow grooves.

## pop
Related genres: indie pop, dance pop, top 40, radio hits, k-pop, synth pop, mainstream.
Known for: catchy hooks, upbeat choruses, polished production, sing-along songs.

## indie pop
Related genres: pop, indie, alternative, bedroom pop, dream pop, indie rock.
Known for: catchy but less polished than mainstream pop, bright guitars and synths, feel-good.

## rock
Related genres: hard rock, alternative, punk, metal, indie rock, classic rock, guitar music.
Known for: electric guitars, drums, loud and driving energy, big choruses.

## metal
Related genres: heavy metal, hard rock, thrash, metalcore, rock, headbanging.
Known for: distorted guitars, very loud and fast, aggressive vocals, heavy drums.

## edm
Related genres: electronic dance music, house, techno, trance, dance, rave, club, festival.
Known for: synths, big drops, four-on-the-floor beat, very danceable, festival energy.

## synthwave
Related genres: retrowave, 80s, outrun, electronic, synth pop, darkwave.
Known for: retro 1980s synths, neon atmosphere, driving beat, night drives.

## hip-hop
Related genres: hip hop, rap, trap, r&b, boom bap, beats.
Known for: rapped vocals, strong beat, bass, swagger, confident lyrics.

## trip-hop
Related genres: downtempo, electronic, hip hop, ambient, dark, moody, chillout.
Known for: slow breakbeats, heavy bass, dark atmospheric textures, late night listening.

## r&b
Related genres: rnb, rhythm and blues, soul, neo soul, slow jams, hip hop, pop.
Known for: smooth vocals, romantic lyrics, groovy mid-tempo beats, date night.

## latin
Related genres: reggaeton, salsa, cumbia, bachata, latin pop, tropical, dance.
Known for: Spanish lyrics, percussion, guitar, very danceable, playful and warm rhythms.

## reggae
Related genres: dub, ska, dancehall, island, caribbean, tropical, beach music.
Known for: offbeat guitar skank, laid-back groove, bass, sunny carefree vibe.
