# Mood Guide

Each section is one retrievable chunk. The heading is the exact mood label used in
`data/songs.csv`. "Related" lists words listeners use for the same feeling, so a
request like "mellow" can find songs tagged "chill" or "relaxed".

## chill
Related moods: relaxed, focused, calm, mellow, laid-back, easygoing, cozy.
Good for: studying, reading, working, rainy days, late nights, background music, winding down.
Typical sound: low to medium energy, slow tempo, soft, often acoustic or lo-fi.

## relaxed
Related moods: chill, calm, mellow, peaceful, unwinding, laid-back, cozy, soothing.
Good for: coffee shops, Sunday mornings, dinner, reading, slow evenings, rainy days.
Typical sound: low energy, gentle tempo, warm, often acoustic.

## focused
Related moods: chill, concentrated, steady, calm, productive, in the zone.
Good for: studying, coding, homework, deep work, writing, exam prep.
Typical sound: low to medium energy, steady beat, few vocals, unobtrusive.

## happy
Related moods: cheerful, joyful, upbeat, sunny, feel-good, bright, positive, carefree, playful.
Good for: mornings, road trips, parties, summer, celebrating, cleaning the house.
Typical sound: medium to high energy, bright, positive, danceable.

## carefree
Related moods: happy, easygoing, breezy, sunny, laid-back, vacation, playful.
Good for: beach days, summer afternoons, vacations, barbecues, hanging out with friends.
Typical sound: medium energy, bright, relaxed groove.

## playful
Related moods: fun, flirty, cheeky, happy, carefree, lively, bouncy.
Good for: dancing, parties, cooking with friends, summer nights.
Typical sound: medium to high energy, bright, very danceable, groovy rhythm.

## euphoric
Related moods: ecstatic, elated, hyped, uplifting, rush, festival, happy, energetic.
Good for: clubs, festivals, raves, peak of a workout, celebrating.
Typical sound: very high energy, fast, bright, electronic, danceable.

## intense
Related moods: powerful, driving, aggressive, pumped, hyped, energetic, fierce, adrenaline.
Good for: workouts, the gym, running, sports, motivation, getting pumped up.
Typical sound: high energy, fast tempo, loud, heavy.

## angry
Related moods: aggressive, furious, rage, heavy, intense, venting, rebellious.
Good for: letting off steam, heavy workouts, venting frustration.
Typical sound: very high energy, very fast, loud, distorted, dark.

## confident
Related moods: bold, swagger, empowered, self-assured, strong, motivated, boss.
Good for: getting ready to go out, walking into an interview, workouts, motivation.
Typical sound: medium to high energy, strong beat, groovy, danceable.

## moody
Related moods: brooding, atmospheric, nocturnal, mysterious, dark, melancholic, cinematic.
Good for: night drives, city lights at night, late-night walks, rainy nights.
Typical sound: medium energy, synths, dark but steady, retro.

## dark
Related moods: moody, gloomy, ominous, shadowy, eerie, brooding, mysterious.
Good for: late nights, night walks, rainy nights, introspection.
Typical sound: low energy, slow, heavy bass, dark and sparse.

## sad
Related moods: melancholic, heartbroken, blue, down, lonely, gloomy, tearful.
Good for: breakups, lonely nights, rainy days, missing someone, crying it out.
Typical sound: low energy, slow tempo, dark, often acoustic.

## melancholic
Related moods: sad, wistful, bittersweet, somber, reflective, nostalgic, gloomy.
Good for: rainy days, autumn, reflection, quiet evenings, grieving.
Typical sound: very low energy, slow, dark, acoustic, often instrumental.

## nostalgic
Related moods: wistful, sentimental, bittersweet, homesick, reminiscing, warm, melancholic.
Good for: looking at old photos, autumn, road trips home, quiet evenings, remembering.
Typical sound: low energy, warm, acoustic, storytelling.

## romantic
Related moods: loving, sensual, tender, intimate, smooth, sweet, date night.
Good for: date night, dinner for two, slow dancing, anniversaries, late nights.
Typical sound: medium energy, smooth, mid-tempo, warm vocals.
